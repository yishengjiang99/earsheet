AI Music Radar Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-03 · Product: AI Music Radar (iOS) · Operator: grepawk / Yisheng Jiang

This policy describes how AI Music Radar handles information. It does not cover other grepawk products.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or receive any personal information.

  • Microphone and audio files: used only to transcribe the music you record or import. Transcription runs entirely on your iPhone. Your audio is never uploaded.
  • Scores and exports: the scores, MIDI, MusicXML and PDF files the app creates are stored only on your device. Deleting them or the app removes them.
  • Diagnostics: if you choose to share crash data with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports.

Sharing

We do not share or sell any information, because we have none. If you share an export through the iOS share sheet, it goes only where you send it.

Web demo

The browser demo at grepawk.com/music-hear also transcribes locally in your browser. Microphone audio is not sent to our server.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
