Tips for better transcriptions

  • Record one instrument or voice at a time, in a quiet room, with the phone close to the source.
  • Full band mixes are harder. Treat the result as a first draft and tidy it in MuseScore or another notation app using the MusicXML export.
  • If the microphone does not work, check Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone > AI Music Radar.
