Hello — thank you for reviewing EarSheet: Music to Sheet Music.

WHAT THE APP DOES
EarSheet is an automatic music transcription app. It listens to music through the iPhone microphone (or analyzes an audio file the user imports) and turns what it hears into written music: an engraved staff score and piano roll, exportable as MIDI, MusicXML, and PDF. No account or sign-in is needed, and none is requested.

HOW TO TEST IT
1. Open the app and tap the microphone button on the main screen.
2. Play a single instrument (piano or guitar work best), sing, or hum a melody.
3. Notes appear on the staff in real time as the sound is analyzed.
4. Tap Play to hear the transcription back with note-by-note highlighting.
5. Tap Save to keep the piece; the Share menu offers MIDI, MusicXML, and PDF export. The free tier saves up to 3 pieces; the Pro paywall appears when saving a 4th piece or choosing a Pro export, and adds unlimited pieces and full exports.

HOW IT WORKS — ALL PROCESSING IS ON THE DEVICE
Transcription is performed by a neural network that runs locally on the iPhone (Core ML). The model is adapted from Spotify's open-source "Basic Pitch" research model, used under its license and credited in the app and its open-source notices; the app, its notation rendering, and its playback engine are original native Swift code. The app contains NO integration with ChatGPT, OpenAI, or any other hosted AI service, and no audio or user content is transmitted anywhere for transcription — there is no transcription server. You can verify this by testing in Airplane Mode: transcription works fully offline.

WHY THIS APP IS NOT A DUPLICATE (GUIDELINE 4.3)
This app turns SOUND into notation. The developer's other music app, Sheet Music Reader by SheetCam, does the inverse: it turns PHOTOGRAPHS of printed sheet music into playback (optical music recognition). The two apps share no code, no models, and no input method — one uses the microphone and an audio-transcription model, the other the camera and an image-recognition pipeline — and they serve different tasks: capturing music you hear or play vs. reading music printed on paper. Within the broader category, EarSheet's combination is distinct: real-time while-you-play transcription with a live engraved score (not only a piano roll), synchronized playback highlighting, and fully offline, on-device processing with no account.
