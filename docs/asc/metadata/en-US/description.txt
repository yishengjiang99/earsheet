Play it, see it. AI Music Radar listens as you play, sing, or hum — and writes the score live, right on your iPhone.

Tap the mic and notes appear while you play: an engraved staff page with tempo, time signature, and key detection. Press play to hear it back with every note lighting up as it sounds, or flip to the piano roll to check pitches and rhythm. Import an audio file or grab the sound straight from a video in your Photos. Then take it further in MuseScore, Dorico, Sibelius, Finale, Logic, GarageBand, or any DAW.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Transcription runs entirely on your device with an on-device neural network. Your recordings are never uploaded.
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking.
• Works offline: on a plane, in a practice room, in a classroom with no Wi-Fi.

MADE FOR
• Students working out a melody by ear or checking their ear training

• Songwriters capturing an idea as notes before it slips away
• Cover musicians and arrangers who want a first draft instead of hours of transcribing

TIPS FOR THE BEST RESULTS
Transcription is most accurate on a single instrument or voice in a quiet room: solo piano, guitar, a sung or hummed melody, one lead line. Full band mixes are harder — tidy the score in your notation app. Think of every result as a fast first draft.
