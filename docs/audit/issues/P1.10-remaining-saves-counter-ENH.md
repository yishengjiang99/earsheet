**Flow:** P1 Free-tier limits & paywall triggers
**Type:** enhancement (upgrade-prompt opportunity, product decision with default below)

### Why (facts from the audit)
Free users learn about the 3-piece limit only when the 4th take is held. A visible counter sets expectations and is a low-pressure upgrade surface.

### Proposed change
Small header row in My Takes for non-Pro users ("2 of 3 free pieces"); at 3/3 the row links to the paywall via the policy gate. Hidden for Pro.

Constraints: no feature flags (ships always-on); analytics anonymous (allow-listed scalar props only, no PII, no titles/audio/notes); never shown to Pro users; user-initiated entries never count toward automatic-prompt caps.
