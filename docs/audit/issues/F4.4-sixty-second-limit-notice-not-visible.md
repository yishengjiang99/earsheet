### Expected
At 60 s the user sees why the take ended, on the screen they land on.

### Actual
`stop(dueToLimit:)` sets `notice` while `phase` is `.writing`; the alert belongs to `ListeningView`, which `onSaved` closes (cover dismissed) as soon as the take is added, so the alert is dismissed with it. [RUNTIME]

### Location
`Sources/App/ListeningView.swift:136-138,315-317,318-325`; presenter `Sources/App/LibraryView.swift:139-153`.
Root cause: Notice state lives in the session that is torn down on success.

### Repro
Record until the 1:00 mark (fake recorder or real). After the take opens no message is visible. [RUNTIME]

Repro rate: needs device

### Severity
P3 — Polish; user may think the app cut them off randomly.

### Suggested fix
Return the notice with the saved take (e.g. `onSaved(take, notice)`) and show it in `LibraryView`/take screen via `library.postNotice` after the cover dismisses.
