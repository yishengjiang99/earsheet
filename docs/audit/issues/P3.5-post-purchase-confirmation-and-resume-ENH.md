**Step ID:** P3.5
**Flow:** P3 Purchase success
**Type:** enhancement (upgrade-prompt opportunity, product decision with default below)

### Why (facts from the audit)
After buying from a locked MIDI/MusicXML row the paywall just closes; the user must find and tap the export again (`ExportView.swift:131-139`). Trial users are not told when billing starts.

### Proposed change
Store the pending action (enum) when the paywall opens from an export row; on `isPro` perform it and show a one-line banner ("Pro is on. Trial ends <date>"). Anonymous event only.

Constraints: no feature flags (ships always-on); analytics anonymous (allow-listed scalar props only, no PII, no titles/audio/notes); never shown to Pro users; user-initiated entries never count toward automatic-prompt caps.

Sources/App/ExportView.swift, Sources/App/PaywallView.swift
