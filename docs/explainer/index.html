Live notes, on-device transcription, real notation

Three things AI Music Radar puts together in one app.

01 · LIVE

Notes while you play

Notes appear on the staff while you play, sing or hum. The model is polyphonic, so chords show up too. The web demo runs at about 0.7 s latency.

02 · ON-DEVICE

Private by architecture

The Core ML model (Spotify Basic Pitch) runs on your iPhone. Transcription works offline, there is no sign-in, and your recordings are never uploaded.

03 · REAL OUTPUT

Made for musicians

An engraved staff with tempo, meter and key detection, playback with note highlighting, and MIDI, MusicXML and PDF export for MuseScore, Dorico, Sibelius, Finale, Logic or GarageBand.

Transcription accuracy, under active tuning

The model is Spotify's Basic Pitch (ICASSP 2022 weights), pinned in models.lock with SHA-256 verification, shipped as versioned release assets, never in git.

Recent work tuned the decoder, not just the model (onset 0.7, frame 0.4, min note 5 frames), lifting note accuracy on real recordings with no retraining. Next: real dynamics. An audit found velocity was tracking model confidence, not loudness; a per-pitch energy calibration now beats both the old estimate and a trained neural head.
