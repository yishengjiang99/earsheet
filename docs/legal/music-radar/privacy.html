AI Music Radar Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-22 (updated) · Product: AI Music Radar (iOS/iPadOS 17+) · Publisher: GrepAwk LLC

This policy covers the AI Music Radar app for iPhone and iPad and its website, music.grepawk.com, both published by GrepAwk LLC. It does not cover other GrepAwk products.

Summary

The AI Music Radar app does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or contacts, and we do not sell data. Music transcription runs on your device, and no audio leaves your iPhone or iPad. The app does send our server a small amount of anonymous usage data and a device record (described below), which are tied only to a random install ID created on your device (not to your name, email address or Apple ID) and are never used for advertising or tracking. You can turn usage data off in the app’s Settings. The website is different in two ways, described under This website: if you buy or join the waitlist we keep your email address, and it uses X and Reddit advertising pixels to measure our ads.

Microphone and audio

  • The app asks for microphone access only so it can listen while you record a take.
  • Recordings and audio files you import are processed on your device by a built-in machine-learning model. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Takes, notes and exports stay on your device until you delete them or choose to share them (for example with the iOS share sheet). Anything you share goes where you send it, under that service’s terms.

Anonymous usage data and device record (app)

  • Usage events. The app records feature events: app opened, transcription started or stopped (with the source, such as microphone, import or video, the length in seconds, the note count and the result), paywall viewed, and the outcome of a purchase or restore (with the product). Each event carries a random install ID created on your device, a random per-session ID, the app version and a timestamp. It never includes audio, notes, titles, file names or anything you type. Turn it off with Settings → Share anonymous usage data; switching it off also deletes events still waiting to be sent.
  • Device record. To keep the service working, the app registers the same random install ID with our server along with the iOS version, app version and build, device model, language/region setting and time zone. If you allow notifications, the push token is stored as well, so we can send them.
  • Purchases. When you buy or restore Pro, the app sends Apple’s signed transaction to our server to confirm the purchase, keyed by an anonymous account token, not your Apple ID.
  • This data is stored on our server without your IP address. Your IP address is used only briefly to limit abusive request rates.

What we do not do

  • The app has no account or sign-in, so the app gives us no names, emails or passwords.
  • The app has no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites. The app’s anonymous usage data goes only to our own server.
  • No sale of personal data. The website’s ad measurement is described below.

This website (music.grepawk.com)

The sections above cover the app. This website also counts anonymous page views and clicks on its own links (for example the web demo button) so we can see which pages are useful. Each event records the page, the referring website's domain, the link clicked and a random ID that lasts only for your browser tab session. It uses no cookies and no third-party analytics, and the events are stored without your IP address. Browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are not counted.

  • Purchases. Checkout is run by Stripe. Stripe collects your email address and payment details under Stripe’s privacy policy; we never see your card number. We keep your email address, the plan you bought and the Stripe purchase reference so we can give you Pro and help with support.
  • App Store waitlist. If you join the waitlist we keep your email address and send you one email when the app launches. To be removed, email us at the address below.
  • Ad campaign links. If you arrive from an ad or a campaign link, the page keeps its campaign tags (utm_source and similar) and the ad click ID (X’s twclid or Reddit’s rdt_cid) in your browser’s local storage for 30 days. They are passed with your checkout or waitlist sign-up so we can tell which campaign it came from.
  • Advertising pixels. To measure our ads, the website may load the X (Twitter) Pixel and the Reddit Pixel. They tell X and Reddit that a visit, a waitlist sign-up, a free trial or a purchase happened, and those companies may set their own cookies and use the data under their own policies (X, Reddit). When you start a trial or buy, our server also reports the conversion to X and Reddit through their Conversions APIs, with the ad click ID and a one-way SHA-256 hash of your email address (never the email itself), the plan value and the time, so they can match it to an ad click.
  • Your choice. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the pixels are not loaded, the click IDs are not sent with your checkout, and our server does not report your purchase to X or Reddit. You can also opt out in X’s and Reddit’s ad settings.

Apple

Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous App Store and crash statistics if you have chosen to share them with developers in your device settings. Apple’s privacy policy governs that data.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and the website is not directed to children under 13. The anonymous usage data and device record described above are handled the same way for every user.
