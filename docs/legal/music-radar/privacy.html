AI Music Radar Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-03 · Product: AI Music Radar (iOS/iPadOS 17+) · Publisher: GrepAwk LLC

This policy covers the AI Music Radar app for iPhone and iPad, published by GrepAwk LLC. It does not cover other GrepAwk products.

Summary

AI Music Radar does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. Music transcription runs on your device, and no audio leaves your iPhone or iPad.

Microphone and audio

  • The app asks for microphone access only so it can listen while you record a take.
  • Recordings and audio files you import are processed on your device by a built-in machine-learning model. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Takes, notes and exports stay on your device until you delete them or choose to share them (for example with the iOS share sheet). Anything you share goes where you send it, under that service’s terms.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, so we hold no names, emails or passwords.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of data.

Apple

Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous App Store and crash statistics if you have chosen to share them with developers in your device settings. Apple’s privacy policy governs that data.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
